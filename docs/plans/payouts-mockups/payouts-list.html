Paid control by permission

With payouts.write on an open tournament the Paid cell is a checkbox, and ticking it saves at once (PATCH). Without payouts.write, or once the tournament is complete, it is text with a check mark: "✓ Paid" or "Not paid". Never a disabled checkbox.

A save that fails (for example the tournament completed in the meantime) un-ticks the box and shows a Toast: "This tournament is complete. Payouts are locked."
