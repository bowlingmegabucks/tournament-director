TV page, layout A: continuous scroll

Option A. One tall list of every division and session, scrolling up at a steady pace and starting over. In the real page the track is the list plus a copy of it, so the loop has no jump. The frame is 16:9, at the size of a TV. Notice strip at the top; the updated table has a gold outline for a few seconds. This mockup scrolls on its own (unless your system asks for less motion).
